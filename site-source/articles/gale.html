A cloaked figure makes her way through a pitch black world, filled with invisible obstacles. When the wind blows, the negative space reveals their shape and form so she can progress.

Download:

Game can be downloaded from itch.io : 🔗

The installation version was for two people to cooperate, one using a joystick to control the character, the other using rotating lava rock + button to control the wind. The downloadable version is controlled using the keyboard.

Created for the Isle of Games 002 (see isleofgames.is) exhibition on the theme of “The Tempest”.
